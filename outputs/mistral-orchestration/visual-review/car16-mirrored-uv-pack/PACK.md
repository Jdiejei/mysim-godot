# car16 mirrored-UV visual review pack

Purpose: let a human reviewer judge each pending mirrored-UV fix for car16 WITHOUT
running the game. Each row gets three panels: the authored source-atlas island, the
current production in-game state (leg A), and the candidate-corrected in-game state
(leg B). Generated by `tools/carbody_visual_pack.py` (read-only; PIL+numpy composition
of existing local evidence). Machine-readable: `metrics.json`.

exactly the candidate surfaces, nothing else.

## The mapping being judged

Production default (CarLoader.CarShader car flip policy): `uv_scale=(-1,-1), offset=(1,1)`,
i.e. `(u,v) -> (1-u, 1-v)`. Extra U flip = mirrored logos/numbers (H1, established turn-2).

Staged candidate per row below: `uv_scale=(1,-1), offset=(0,1)`, i.e. `(u,v) -> (u, 1-v)`.
U direction restored, V image-origin correction kept - the same family as the three
already-promoted car16 ext repairs (BODY/FRONTBUMPER/REARBUMPER ext; runs 41572315488,
41571290602) and the 11 promoted car20 roles (run 41453316712).

## How to read a row image

Left panel: SOURCE (authored) - the exact atlas region the surface samples (UV0 bounds,
wrapped sampling for negative V), so text/logos read as authored.
Middle: IN-GAME BEFORE (leg A, production) / right: IN-GAME AFTER (leg B, candidates),
same screen rectangle.

Verdict protocol per row: PASS iff the AFTER glyph chirality and reading direction match
the SOURCE panel (fix = texture remap only; geometry does not move). Additional check for
the strut rows: foreign-patch sampling ("wrong-island" artwork) must disappear in AFTER.
Rows marked no-change need the separate decision below.

overlap on screen in that capture, so rows 1/3/5 all cite the same before/after crop. The
per-row SOURCE panel still gives each row its own authored-orientation reference, and the

which glyph belongs to which surface. Same for the right view and the front badge cluster.

## No-change rows (7, 10) - camera-independent evidence

EXHAUST ext and REARBUMPER badges produced no measurable pixel change at the five exterior
plus cockpit cameras. The staged remap is still a real orientation fix for atlas regions
those cameras do not resolve at 960x540. Decision options for the reviewer, both documented:
(a) accept from family evidence - identical (u,1-v) remap already validated per-renderer on
the same car's promoted surfaces (41572315488, 41571290602) and car20's 11 roles (41453316712);
or (b) require a targeted close-up leg before promotion of these two rows only.

## Limits

- 960x540 downscaled captures: judge ORIENTATION, not sharpness or color.
- UV bounds are per-renderer vertex-extent (decal meshes are small); the atlas crops show
  the exact sampled region under wrapped sampling, not the whole texture.
- Cockpit-view diff (33903px) has shared attribution; do not score single rows from it alone.
- No acceptance claim is made here; verdicts are the human reviewer's, per the P1 rules.
